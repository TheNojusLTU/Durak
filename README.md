# Durak

Durak is a turn-based card game in which players try to get rid of all their cards. The last player holding cards is the **durak**, or "fool".

## How to play

The game uses a standard 36-card deck, from six through ace.

1. Each player is dealt six cards.
2. One card is revealed to determine the trump suit. The remaining deck becomes the draw pile.
3. The attacker plays one or more cards against the defender.
4. The defender must beat every attacking card with a higher card of the same suit or with a trump card. A trump card can only be beaten by a higher trump card.
5. Other players may add cards whose ranks match cards already on the table, up to the number of cards the defender held at the start of the attack.
6. If the defender beats every card, the cards are discarded. If not, the defender picks up all cards on the table.
7. Players draw back up to six cards, beginning with the attacker and continuing clockwise.

The attack passes to the next player after a successful defense. If the defender picks up, the next attack is made by the player to the defender's left.
